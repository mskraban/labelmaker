Printing tips

  1. Set the paper size in the print dialog to match the sheet (A4 or Letter).
  2. Turn off headers, footers and “fit to page”; use scale 100%.
  3. Do a test print on plain paper first and hold it to the light against a label sheet.
  4. For a freezer, use a second line such as “Date: ____” and write the date with a permanent marker.

Questions

Is it free and private?

Yes. Nothing is uploaded: your list is processed in your browser, and it works offline once loaded.

Do I need to buy label sheets?

No. Choose a plain paper grid, switch on cutting outlines, print and cut. Self-adhesive sheets just save the cutting.

Why are my labels slightly misaligned?

Printers add small margins of their own. Print at 100% and use the “Shift right / down” fields to correct by a fraction of a millimetre.

Can I make round or square labels?

Yes. Pick one of the “Plain A4” sheets, then choose Circle, Square, Oval, Pill or Rounded rectangle under “Label shape”. Switch on cutting outlines and cut along the line. Die-cut sheets (Avery and similar) keep the shape of the sticker.

I do not have colour ink. Can I print black and white?

Yes: tick “Black & white”. The preview and the print use white paper and black ink, dark themes are inverted, and patterns become light grey.

Can I change the font or add a date?

Yes. Choose from 16 fonts (including handwriting, typewriter and marker styles) and adjust size and capitals. Under “Date” you can add a write-in line, today's date, a date you pick, or a packed and use-by pair such as “+3 months”.

Can I use my own sheet size?

Yes: choose “Custom sheet…” and enter the label size, how many across and down, and the margins.

What do the icons mean?

They are picked from the words on each label (a leaf for herbs, a wheat ear for flour, a snowflake for the freezer). Add “| second line | icon” to a line to force one of the built-in icons.
